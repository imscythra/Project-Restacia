# Privacy Policy for Restacia

**Effective Date:** April 21, 2025  
**Developer:** Scythra  
**Contact:** imscythra@outlook.com

## 1. Overview

Restacia is a centralized virtual mental health platform and psychology sidekick designed to support users through interactive activities, helpful articles, and an AI-powered chatbot.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Restacia handles your data. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

---

## 2. Information We Do **Not** Collect

Restacia does **not** collect any of the following:

- Personally identifiable information (e.g. name, email address, IP address)
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Access to your camera, microphone, or storage

We do not require users to sign in, create accounts, or provide personal information to use the app.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

Restacia integrates certain third-party services that may collect anonymous or aggregated data. These include:

- **OpenAI API** – used to power the in-app AI chatbot  
- **Spotify Player (Embedded)** – may collect anonymous playback data according to Spotify’s policies  
- **WebView2 (Article Viewer)** – displays web content, which may include third-party sites that collect usage data

Please refer to the respective privacy policies of these services:

- [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy)  
- [Spotify Privacy Policy](https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy)

---

## 4. Data Storage and Sharing

Restacia does not store or transmit any user data to external servers controlled by us.  
We do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

Restacia is intended for general audiences, including those seeking mental health support or interested in psychology. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from minors.
